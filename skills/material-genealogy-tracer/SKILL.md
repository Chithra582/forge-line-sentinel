# Material Genealogy Tracer Skill

## Overview
Maps component relationships from raw supplier heat numbers to finished serialized assemblies for complete traceability.

## Operations
1. Links consumed lot numbers with parent assembly serials at each station.
2. Audits assembly history to detect deviation causes.
3. Executes forward and reverse trace queries in under 5 seconds during quality audits.
